# Wifi Uptime Meter - Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 23, 2026

Wifi Uptime Meter does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data. Period.

## What the app does with data

Session details you enter (airline, flight, seat, price, notes, contact fields) and recorded session results are stored only on your Mac, in your local Application Support folder. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no cloud sync.

## Network access

The app's only network activity is its connectivity checks: while a session is recording, it periodically requests one of three public reachability endpoints (gstatic.com, captive.apple.com, cloudflare.com) to determine whether your connection is working. These requests contain no personal information.

## Emails and posts

When you use "Open email draft" or "Open X post," the app hands the drafted text to your own mail client or browser. Nothing is sent by the app itself, and we never see the content.

## Deleting your data

Quit the app and delete the folder Library/Containers/com.chrisler.wifimeter in your home folder. That is all the data there is.
